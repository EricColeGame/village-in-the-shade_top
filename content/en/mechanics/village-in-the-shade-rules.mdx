Kagatsu Village looks peaceful in the daylight, but eight old laws quietly decide when you can leave your house, where the story will let you walk, and which chapters ever open at all. ## The Eight Village in the Shade Rules at a Glance

Kagatsu's code is a list of eight rules, and the currently documented English versions are working translations rather than final official localization. According to the [documented Kagatsu Village rules list](https://villageintheshade.com/village/rules), the wording may change once the official interface text ships — but the underlying structure has been stable across player reports.

| 2 | Cooperate for the good of the village | Whole game | Low — theme, not a hidden meter |

## What Each Village in the Shade Rule Means in Practice

### Rule One: The 11:00 PM Curfew

Rule One is the clearest mechanical rule in the game. Under normal circumstances, hitting 11:00 PM sends you back home rather than letting you roam. On eligible nights, special events let you step outside after curfew — and that is the doorway into ghosts, Magatama, Lost Books, and the other night-only systems.

If you are chasing nighttime collectibles, Rule One is not a restriction to obey. It is a schedule to exploit.

### Rules Two, Three, and Four: Values, Not Hidden Meters

Rules Two through Four read like village etiquette, and that is exactly how they should be treated until a story scene explicitly frames something as a violation.

- **Rule Two (cooperate):** There is no verified evidence that skipping a single villager request triggers a punishment. Treat it as Kagatsu's social fabric.
- **Rule Three (strangers):** The protagonist is an outsider too, which makes this rule thematically loaded. Ordinary conversations with new NPCs are not automatic violations.
- **Rule Four (secrets):** Friendship progression is not the same as exposing a secret. Villagers volunteering their own information is not framed as a breach either.

Community reports consistently push back on the idea of an invisible reputation bar for these three. Play naturally, and pay attention when the story raises its voice.

### Rules Five and Six: The Autumn Turning Point

Rule Five and Rule Six both land in Autumn, and both are confirmed story beats rather than background flavor. Rule Five ("do not leave the village") is often misread as a ban on every trip to the mountains, hunting grounds, or fishing spots. It is not — the meaningful boundary is narrative and deliberately spoiler-sensitive.

Rule Six ("do not answer voices from the mountain") is the clearest signal that the rules belong to the horror side of the game. It refers to a specific scripted voice, not ambient wind or mining noise. Players who want to minimize scares should pay close attention whenever the story foregrounds that voice.

No reliable equipment checklist exists for these two chapters. Normal tool, money, and supply progression is enough.

### Rules Seven and Eight: Winter Gates and Late-Story Costs

Rule Seven is the most practical late rule because it has a known preparation requirement: the Winter route tied to it needs a **Gold Axe** to clear fallen trees in the thicket. If Winter progress stalls, check your tool tier before anything else. Rule Seven is also frequently confused with Lost Books — those are legitimate nighttime collectibles, not contraband.

Rule Eight ("every wish requires a fitting price") is the broadest and most ominous line on the list. Treat it as late-story meaning until stronger gameplay evidence appears; inventing fixed costs or repeatable wish systems around it is speculation.

## Does This Count as Breaking a Village in the Shade Rule?

Most confusion comes from assuming every ordinary action is being judged. Here is how the common cases actually shake out based on current documentation and player experience.

| Action | Rule players assume it breaks | What is actually documented |
|---|---|---|
| Skipping a villager request | Rule Two | No verified punishment system |
| Chatting with a newcomer | Rule Three | Normal NPC interaction is fine |
| Sharing a friend's backstory | Rule Four | Friendship progress ≠ exposing secrets |
| Hiking to the mountain | Rule Five | Story-specific boundary, not a travel ban |
| Hearing mining ambience | Rule Six | Only the scripted voice counts |
| Grabbing a Lost Book at night | Rule Seven | Lost Books are legitimate collectibles |

If an action is not tied to a scripted scene or the 11:00 PM curfew, it is almost certainly not a violation.

## How the Village in the Shade Rules Unlock Through the Story

The rules are not a checklist you clear in order. The narrative places the protagonist in situations where the code suddenly becomes relevant, which is why preparation matters more than memorization.

| Season / phase | Active rule | What to have ready |
|---|---|---|
| Early game | Rule One | Nothing — just respect the clock |
| Autumn | Rule Five | Standard tools, money, supplies |
| Autumn | Rule Six | No gear; attention to scripted scenes |
| Winter | Rule Seven | Gold Axe for the thicket |
| Late story | Rule Eight | Story context, not equipment |

Two practical habits cover nearly all of it. First, keep your axe upgraded ahead of Winter rather than during it — tool materials are the usual bottleneck. Second, ignore speculative prep lists that are not tied to a verified story step. The Village in the Shade rules reward steady progression, not hoarding.

## Village in the Shade Rules vs. Village in the Sunshine

This is the biggest system difference in the game, and it changes how much of Kagatsu you actually see.

| Feature | Village in the Shade | Village in the Sunshine |
|---|---|---|
| Normal rule-breaking | Possible | Blocked |
| 11:00 PM curfew | Core mechanic and night access | Alters access to the hidden path |
| Night-only content | Ghosts, Magatama, Lost Books | Shifted toward safer rural life |
| Overall tone | Mystery and horror | Relaxed village simulation |

In Village in the Shade, the rules are part of the mystery path. In Village in the Sunshine, the game leans into a safer rural-life route and removes normal rule-breaking. Neither is "correct" — but if you want the hidden story, you need the Shade route, and you need to tolerate the curfew.

## FAQ: Village in the Shade Rules

**How many Village in the Shade rules are there?**
Eight are documented. Rule One covers the 11:00 PM curfew, Rules Five and Six are Autumn story chapters, Rule Seven is a Winter chapter with a Gold Axe requirement, and Rule Eight carries late-story meaning.

**Do the Village in the Shade rules carry over to Village in the Sunshine?**
No. Sunshine mode removes normal rule-breaking and redirects access to the hidden story path, so the code functions very differently there.

**What is the most important Village in the Shade rules prep step?**
Upgrade your axe to Gold before Winter. Rule Seven's route needs it to clear fallen trees in the thicket, and a stalled Winter chapter is almost always a tool problem.

**Can I ignore the rules on a first playthrough?**
You can ignore the thematic ones, but Rule One, Rule Five, Rule Six, and Rule Seven are wired into progression. Follow the story's cues, keep your tools current, and the rest of Kagatsu's code will take care of itself.
