## Rin, Komako, and the Young Core of Kagatsu

The heart of the cast is a group of villagers who are barely older than your own character. That's not an accident. Kagatsu is a place where responsibility gets handed out early, and the youngest residents carry most of it.

**Rin** runs the entire village despite still being in her late teens. She's fiercely responsible, fixated on money, and rigid about the village code — traits that make her read as overbearing before you understand how much pressure she's under. Her line about work and pay is pure Rin: blunt, transactional, and a little exhausting. She's also the character most likely to gate your progress, so staying on her good side early pays off.

**Komako** is the opposite first impression. Gentle, a little timid, and genuinely kind, she's the villager you meet first and functions as your introduction to how Kagatsu works. She's also an extremely capable hunter — and she has no memory of "the incident," which is the kind of detail that tends to matter later.

**Shiroji**, her older brother, is a burly, quiet lumberjack who puts everything into his work. He worries about Komako constantly and, notably, once offered a treasure to a sacred beast. Getting him to open up takes patience, but he's friendlier than his monosyllables suggest.

**Kisuke** is the village carpenter and the best at what he does — a fact he'll remind you of. He drinks too much for someone who can't hold his liquor, gets loud, and needles you with jokes about mistaking you for a nail. Underneath the bluster is someone who lost a younger sibling and never quite dealt with it.

**Yuta** is the easygoing one: lots of hobbies, no visible ambition, and a habit of redirecting any question about his past. He calls himself your "bro" within minutes of meeting you. That friendliness may be genuine — or it may be a very good cover.

**Sazanka** runs the general store, skips work whenever she can, and tinkers with machines in her spare time. She's also hunting a lost treasure and tends to act before she thinks when something excites her. She's one of the most useful characters to befriend early, since she controls a chunk of your supply chain.

The older half of the roster fills in the village's practical needs — food, medicine, goods, and paperwork — while quietly adding to the mystery.

| Nago | Nothing the villagers want | Trying to buy land for solar panels |
| Konno | Access to Rin | Sarcastic, wealthy tastes, unclear motives |

A few stand out. **Rokkaku** is laid-back to the point of laziness, but he's been in Kagatsu for a decade and clearly knows more than he lets on. **Yoh**, his adopted daughter, is shy and lonely with few children her age around — and she shares his awareness of the mountain voice, which makes the two of them a matched pair of clues.

**Hasumi** is the quiet, intellectual physician who compounds her own medicines and delivers one of the more chilling lines in the cast about poison costing you your life. **Sumire**, her younger sibling, is bright and practical, saves money well, forages effectively, and is learning archery from Komako with a very specific target in mind.

Then there are the outsiders. **Chinana** sells goods out of her truck and is friendly in a way that never quite lets you get a read on her. **Nago** is the polite city hall employee nobody listens to. And **Konno**, Rin's guardian and the village head's secretary, is eccentric, sarcastic, and apparently watching the village — a phrase the game dangles without explaining.

**Tobari** deserves special attention. A teenager who openly hates Kagatsu, breaks the code on purpose, and might be the next sacrifice is not a background NPC — that's a plot engine. Tobari's warning about the village "infecting" you is one of the few moments where a character speaks directly to the game's central dread.

The pattern across these entries is worth noting: treasure shows up three separate times (Shiroji, Sazanka, and the sacred beast connection), memory loss appears once, and the sacrifice thread ties directly to the village code Rin enforces so rigidly. That's not coincidence — it's structure.

## How to Get to Know the Village in the Shade Characters

There's no shortcut to understanding this cast — the writing rewards attention more than optimization. That said, a few habits make the process much smoother.

- **Talk to everyone daily, including the people with nothing to sell.** Nago and Yoh don't run shops, but they carry story information.
- **Read the bullet points as foreshadowing.** Every character profile in the official material lists three traits; the third one is almost always the plot hook.
- **Prioritize Rin, Sazanka, and Hasumi early.** They gate progress, supplies, and healing respectively.
- **Don't write off the rude characters.** Kisuke and Tobari both give you information that politer villagers won't.
- **Track treasure mentions.** Sazanka's lost treasure, Shiroji's offering, and the sacred beast thread appear to connect.
- **Keep notes on ages.** The generational gaps — who lost a sibling, who adopted a child, who arrived ten years ago — line up in ways that are easy to miss in a single play session.

Community reports from players working through the game suggest that the villagers who seem least relevant in the first few hours tend to become central later, particularly Yoh and Konno. Treat early conversations as setup rather than filler.

## FAQ

**Who are the main Village in the Shade characters?**

Rin, the young village head, and Komako, the hunter you meet first, anchor the early story, with Shiroji, Kisuke, Yuta, and Sazanka forming the young core. The adult cast — Rokkaku, Hasumi, Chinana, Konno, and Nago — carries most of the village's secrets.

**Which Village in the Shade characters should I befriend first?**

Start with Rin for access and rules, Sazanka for supplies, and Hasumi for medicine. After that, invest time in Komako and Shiroji, since their family thread connects to several of the game's larger mysteries.

**Is Tobari really the next sacrifice?**

The official character material flags Tobari as a possible next sacrifice, but it's presented as an open question rather than a confirmed fact. Tobari's open contempt for Kagatsu and habit of breaking the village code make the character a likely focal point for that storyline.

**Can I customize my own character in Village in the Shade?**

Yes. Your character's appearance can be changed with a range of hairstyles, clothing, and accessories, and you can swap the look whenever you like rather than being locked in at the start.

Understanding the **Village in the Shade characters** is really about reading between the lines — the game hands you a friendly hunter, a bossy teenager, and a sarcastic secretary, then quietly tells you that one of them lost their memory, one is under surveillance, and one might be sacrificed. Keep the tables above handy, and the village will start making sense a lot faster.
