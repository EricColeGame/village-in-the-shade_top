### Leadership and rules

Rin heads Kagatsu Village, which makes her the resident to check with when a request or unlock feels blocked. Konno is listed as Rin's guardian, a relationship clue that hints at how the leadership structure works behind the scenes.

### Shops, food, and care

Sazanka runs the general store, so she is your default stop for everyday supplies. Rokkaku runs the diner and is the adoptive guardian of Yoh. Hasumi serves as the village physician, which makes her the person to remember when a long day of hunting goes wrong.

### Hunters, farmers, and outdoor residents

Komako is the first villager the player meets, which gives her an outsized role in the opening hours. She is also named as Sumire's archery teacher, so her skill set ties directly into combat and hunting systems. Shiroji is Komako's older brother.

### Visitors and story-linked residents

Chinana brings goods in from outside the village and is Hasumi's childhood friend. That combination — outside supply line plus a personal link to the physician — makes her one of the more interesting connective characters among the Village in the Shade villagers, especially if you are tracking how the village interacts with the wider world.
