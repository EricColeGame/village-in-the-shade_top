## What Is Village in the Shade?

Village in the Shade is a hand-painted farming and life simulation game set in Kagatsu, a fictional Japanese mountain village that looks idyllic from a distance and feels considerably stranger up close. You plant crops, customize your farm and home, and ease into a slow countryside routine — except the village has rules, and the game is very clear that breaking them has consequences. It is published by NIS America and blends a cozy daily loop with psychological and folk horror undertones.

That combination is exactly why the Village in the Shade PC release has drawn so much attention. Most farming sims ask you to relax; this one asks you to relax *and* pay attention to what the neighbors are doing after dark.

| Element | How it shows up in the game |
|---|---|
| Core loop | Planting crops, tending fields, customizing your farm and home |
| Setting | Kagatsu, a remote Japanese mountain village |
| Art direction | Hand-painted visuals with a storybook quality |
| Tone | Quiet countryside living with a persistent unsettling undercurrent |
| Horror flavor | Folk horror and psychological tension rather than jump scares |
| Central hook | Follow the village's rules — or deal with the fallout |

Reviewers who have spent time with the game have focused on that tension. Coverage from outlets like RPG Fan frames the setting as a critique of insular rural communities — the stasis, the suspicion of outsiders, the slow decay — while other reviews describe the overall experience as cozy farming wrapped around a mystery. In other words, the farming is genuine, not a bait-and-switch, but the story is doing something darker in the background.
