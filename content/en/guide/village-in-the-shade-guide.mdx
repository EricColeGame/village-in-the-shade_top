## What Is Village in the Shade?

Village in the Shade is a single-player farming simulation developed by Nippon Ichi Software, the studio behind the Yomawari horror series, and published in the West by [NIS America](https://nisamerica.com/). In Japan the game is titled *Hono Gurashi no Niwa*, and it takes place in Kagatsu, a secluded mountain village shaped by farming, changing seasons, and traditions passed down through generations.

The daytime loop will feel familiar to anyone who has played a life sim before: clear a neglected plot, plant crops, raise animals, fish, hunt, gather resources, craft furniture, and get to know the people around you. The twist is that Kagatsu runs on a code. Official descriptions warn against talking to strangers, leaving the village, and going outside at night — and breaking those warnings is what opens the story's darker half.

## A Village in the Shade Guide to Your First Week

The opening hours set the tone for everything that follows. Kagatsu rewards slow, steady work rather than a rushed checklist, so the goal for week one is not to see everything — it is to build a base that makes weeks two and three comfortable. A good Village in the Shade guide should tell you what to do first, what to delay, and what to leave alone until you are ready for the consequences.

| Priority | Why it matters | When to tackle it |
|---|---|---|
| Clear and plant your starting field | Crops are your most reliable early income | Days 1–3 |
| Learn the shop and selling routine | A harvest only helps once it converts to money | Days 1–2 |
| Repair and upgrade core tools | Better tools mean faster field work and gathering | First week |
| Unlock fishing and hunting | Adds protein, materials, and a second income stream | First week |
| Start cooking with surplus ingredients | Turns spare produce into useful daily items | Once you have extras |
| Meet every named villager | Relationships gate later help and story beats | Ongoing |
| Test the curfew | Nighttime systems are optional but story-relevant | Only when you are ready |

A few habits that pay off quickly:

- **Keep a buffer of every crop.** Selling 100% of a harvest leaves you without cooking ingredients or gift options later.
- **Bank money before tool upgrades.** Upgrades are a long-term multiplier, but only if you can still afford seeds afterward.
- **Do not ignore the neglected garden.** It is one of the cheapest improvements available to you early.
- **Write down villager names and roles.** Kagatsu has a confirmed cast of 14 named characters, and remembering who does what saves a lot of walking.

## Farming, Livestock, and Money: A Village in the Shade Guide to Daily Life

Second, seasonal festivals and community events exist in the game's structure, but exact dates and rewards are best treated as unconfirmed until you see them in your own save. Community reports and early player experience are still thin on the finer details, so this Village in the Shade guide sticks to what is verifiable and flags the rest.

## Village Rules, the 11 PM Curfew, and Nighttime Exploration

Kagatsu follows eight rules, and an 11 PM curfew shapes what you can safely do after dark. Official descriptions emphasize three warnings above the others: do not talk to strangers, do not leave the village, and do not go outside at night. Breaking the code is not a punishment screen — it is the door to the supernatural side of the story.

| Rule area | What official descriptions say | What breaking it opens |
|---|---|---|
| Strangers | Do not talk to strangers | Encounters tied to outsiders and the village's hidden history |
| Leaving the village | Do not leave Kagatsu | Story threads about what exists beyond the valley |
| Going outside at night | Respect the 11 PM curfew | Nighttime exploration, supernatural encounters, main-story progress |
| The remaining rules | Confirmed to exist as part of the eight | Details surface through play rather than announcements |

Practical advice for your first night out:

- **Decide before you go.** The standard experience lets you break the code, but Village in the Sunshine does not — pick your mode at the start and commit.
- **Treat 11 PM as a hard line.** Plan your evening route so you are either safely home or deliberately out.
- **Go out with a purpose.** Nighttime exploration is where the mystery lives; wandering without a goal mostly costs you daylight stamina the next morning.
- **Expect the tone to shift.** The horror warnings are official, and the atmosphere after curfew is a deliberate contrast to daytime farming.

This is the part of the game where a Village in the Shade guide earns its keep, because the consequences of a broken rule are narrative, not just mechanical. If you want the full mystery, the standard mode is the only path that delivers it.

## Village in the Sunshine vs. the Standard Experience

Village in the Sunshine is selectable at the very beginning of the game. In this mode you can enjoy Kagatsu without being able to break the village code — earlier materials referred to this safer option as Peaceful Life Mode. It is not a difficulty slider; it is a structural choice about which half of the game you experience.

| Feature | Standard experience | Village in the Sunshine |
|---|---|---|
| Breaking village rules | Allowed | Not possible |
| Nighttime exploration | Available after the 11 PM curfew | Restricted by design |
| Supernatural encounters | Part of the main progression | Reduced or absent |
| Horror and mature themes | Present, with official warnings | Largely avoided |
| Farming, livestock, fishing, crafting | Full systems | Full systems |
| Story progression | Mystery-focused main story | Exact differences remain unconfirmed |

How to choose:

- **Pick the standard mode** if you came for the mystery and do not mind being unsettled after dark.
- **Pick Village in the Sunshine** if you want a pure farming and community experience without the horror pressure.
- **Do not assume you can switch mid-story.** Mode selection happens at the start, and the differences in story progression are not fully documented yet.

Either way, the daily life systems are identical, so a Village in the Shade guide focused on farming, money, and villager relationships applies to both modes.

## FAQ

**Is Village in the Shade actually scary?**
It carries official horror warnings, mature themes, and genuine nighttime danger, so yes — the standard experience is designed to be unsettling. Village in the Sunshine exists specifically for players who want the farming without that layer.

**What should I do first in my first week?**
Clear and plant your starting field, learn how selling works, and put money toward tool upgrades before you spend on decoration. The first-week table in this Village in the Shade guide is ordered by what actually compounds over time.

**Can I play without ever breaking the village rules?**
Yes. Choose Village in the Sunshine at the start and you cannot break the code. The trade-off is that the mystery-focused main story and nighttime exploration are effectively closed off.

**Which platforms can I play it on, and when did it release?**
It is available on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2. Steam and Japan launched in late July 2026, with the Western release following on October 27, 2026.
