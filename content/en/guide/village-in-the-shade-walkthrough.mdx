## Why This Village in the Shade Walkthrough Is Different

Kagatsu Village looks like the perfect place to start over — right up until the sun goes down. If you want a Village in the Shade walkthrough that treats both halves of the game seriously, you need a plan that covers the cozy daytime loop of crops, animals, and neighbors alongside the after-dark layer that only opens once you break the village code. Skipping either half means missing what makes this farming sim stand out from the pack.

That matters because the game is not a straight line. A Village in the Shade walkthrough should read like a set of priorities, not a rigid script — the game deliberately lets you decide how deep into the shade you want to walk.

| Walkthrough Phase | Main Focus | Typical Timing |

Developed by Nippon Ichi Software — the studio behind the Yomawari horror series — and published in the West by NIS America, Village in the Shade is a single-player farming simulation set in a secluded mountain village shaped by seasons and traditions. That pedigree explains the tonal split: the farming systems are genuinely relaxing, and the mystery is genuinely unsettling.

### Standard Mode vs. Village in the Sunshine

Village in the Sunshine is selectable at the beginning of the game and lets you enjoy Kagatsu without the ability to break the village code. Earlier materials referred to this safer option as Peaceful Life Mode. The standard experience is the opposite: you can break the rules, explore after curfew, and continue the mystery-focused main story.

| Feature | Standard Experience | Village in the Sunshine |
|---|---|---|
| Break village rules | Yes | No |
| Nighttime exploration | Yes | Locked |
| Supernatural encounters | Yes | Reduced or absent |
| Mystery main story | Full progression | Unconfirmed depth |
| Farming, livestock, fishing | Yes | Yes |
| Best for | Players who want the complete game | Players who want pure cozy farming |

If you are unsure, start in the standard experience and simply follow the rules until you feel ready. Nothing forces you to break curfew on day one, and you can keep farming peacefully for as long as you like.

## A Step-by-Step Village in the Shade Walkthrough for Week One

Your first seven days set the pace for everything after. The goal is not to maximize profit — it is to remove friction so that later systems unlock smoothly.

### Days 1–3: Stabilize the Farm

Start by clearing and planting rather than exploring. You begin with a neglected home and garden, so every early action should either produce income or reduce future chores. Plant the fastest reliable crops, water daily, and resist the urge to spend your first earnings on decoration.

### Days 4–7: Build Income and Unlock Tools

Once crops are in the ground, branch out. Cooking turns raw harvests into higher-value goods, fishing fills the gaps between crop cycles, and a hunting license opens meat and animal materials from the countryside. Tool upgrades are the highest-value early purchase you can make.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clear and plant your starting plot | Creates a daily income rhythm |
| 2 | Water and harvest consistently | Builds the habit the whole game runs on |
| 3 | Cook your first harvests | Turns cheap crops into better sale value |
| 4 | Fish by season, time, and location | Reliable filler income between harvests |
| 5 | Unlock a hunting license | Adds meat and crafting materials |
| 6 | Upgrade your core tools | Saves in-game time every single day |
| 7 | Buy basic furniture in town | Small comfort upgrades, no rush |

This is where most Village in the Shade walkthrough attempts go wrong: players chase the mystery in week one with no income, no tools, and no food buffer, then find the nighttime content punishing. Build the farm first. The shade will wait.

## Village Rules, the 11 PM Curfew, and Nighttime Exploration

Kagatsu follows a code. Official descriptions warn against talking to strangers, leaving the village, and going outside at night, and the community guide hub documents eight rules in total alongside an 11 PM curfew. Only the broad themes are officially described, so treat any specific list of all eight as player experience until you verify it in your own copy.

| Rule Theme | Status |
|---|---|
| Do not talk to strangers | Officially described |
| Do not leave the village | Officially described |
| Do not go outside at night | Officially described |
| Curfew at 11 PM | Reported by the community |
| Full list of eight rules | Community-reported, verify in-game |
| Consequences for breaking rules | Unlocks nighttime systems and story |

Breaking the code is not a fail state — it is the unlock condition for the darker half of the game. Nighttime exploration opens supernatural encounters and story progression tied to Kagatsu's hidden history. Bring supplies, save before you head out, and treat the first few nights as reconnaissance rather than a shopping trip.

If horror is not your thing, Village in the Sunshine removes the temptation entirely. There is no shame in that choice, and the farming loop is identical.

## Farming, Livestock, and Long-Term Progression

Once week one is behind you, the game opens up. The systems below are the ones worth investing time into, roughly in order of return.

| System | What to Focus On | Conditions That Matter |
|---|---|---|
| Crops | Fields, ridges, rice paddies, crop quality | Season and weather |
| Fertilizer and scarecrows | Quality boosts and crop protection | Unlocked through progression |
| Livestock | Daily care, affection, animal products | Village evaluations |
| Fishing | Seasonal and time-based catches | Location and time of day |
| Hunting | Meat and animal materials | License required |
| Mining and crafting | Tool upgrades and home objects | Tool access by area |
| Cooking | Higher-value goods from raw ingredients | Recipe unlocks |
| Villager bonds | Relationships and story notes | Confirmed 14 named characters |

A few practical habits separate smooth playthroughs from messy ones:

- **Check the season before buying seeds.** Seasonal crops are the backbone of your income, and off-season planting wastes both money and days.
- **Feed and pet animals before you leave the farm.** Affection and daily care feed directly into product quality and village evaluations.
- **Keep one save before any rule-breaking night.** Nighttime content is where surprises live.
- **Do not assume romance or marriage.** Bonds with villagers can deepen, but romance, marriage, and children should not be expected until officially confirmed.

Village events and traditional Japanese festivals appear as the seasons turn, and shops stock furniture you can place at home. Furniture is cosmetic-leaning, so fund it after tools and animals, not before.

## FAQ

### How long does a full Village in the Shade walkthrough take?

It depends heavily on mode and pacing. Village in the Sunshine is a straightforward farming run, while the standard experience adds nighttime exploration and a mystery main story that stretches the playtime considerably. Because the game is built around gradual, season-by-season progress rather than a fixed checklist, most players will spend far more time on farm expansion than on the story beats themselves.

### Can I follow a Village in the Shade walkthrough in Village in the Sunshine?

Partly. Every farming, fishing, livestock, and crafting step applies to both modes, but anything involving curfew, rule-breaking, or nighttime exploration does not. If you are playing the safer mode, ignore the nighttime sections of any Village in the Shade walkthrough and focus on farm development and villager bonds.

### What should I do first in week one?

Clear and plant your starting plot, water daily, cook your first harvests, unlock a hunting license, and upgrade your core tools. Do not chase the mystery before you have income and supplies — the nighttime content is much harder without a functioning farm behind you.

### Is the horror content avoidable?

Yes. Village in the Sunshine lets you enjoy Kagatsu without the ability to break the village code, so the supernatural layer stays closed. The standard experience is where curfew-breaking, nighttime encounters, and the mystery storyline live, and you can delay all of it for as long as you want by simply following the rules.
